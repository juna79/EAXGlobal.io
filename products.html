Kweli Verify
Available now

Document integrity and authenticity.

Confirms a record is the genuine, unaltered file issued by a known organisation — proving integrity, provenance and issuer authenticity. It does not prove that the information inside is true.

See on kweli.solutions

Kweli Claims
Pilot focus

An integrity layer for insurance claims.

An independent way to verify that claims evidence — invoices, assessors’ reports, medical documents — is genuine and unaltered, without replacing the claims-management systems already in use.

Kweli Supply
In development

Trust for supply chains and trade.

Intended to let each party verify that the documents they receive — invoices, delivery notes, certificates, inspection and export records — are the genuine records issued upstream.

Kweli Credentials
Future product

Verification for certificates and licences.

The same trust layer extends to certificates, licences, professional credentials and institutional documents — shown as part of the direction, not an immediate commercial focus.

EAX Marketplace
Long-term direction

A trusted digital marketplace, built toward.

A marketplace where organisations, documents and transactions are verifiable by design. This is being explored and built toward — it is not an operating marketplace today.
