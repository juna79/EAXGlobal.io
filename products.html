Pilot focus

An integrity layer for insurance claims.

Invoices, estimates and assessor reports from different parties converge into one claim — each verified on the trust layer before the claim is reviewed and approved. Without replacing the claims systems already in use.

In development

Trust for supply chains and trade.

A record travels the whole journey — manufacturer, certificate, shipment, port, buyer — and every party can verify it is the genuine one issued upstream. Continuous, global, verifiable.
