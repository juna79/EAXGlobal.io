Kweli Verify
Available now

Document integrity and authenticity.

Confirms a record is the genuine, unaltered file issued by a known organisation — proving integrity, provenance and issuer authenticity. It does not prove that the information inside is true.

See on kweli.solutions

Kweli Claims
Pilot focus

An integrity layer for insurance claims.

Claims rest on documents from many parties. Kweli Claims verifies that each — invoices, assessors’ reports, medical documents — is genuine and unaltered, assembling a trusted claim without replacing the claims systems already in use.

Kweli Supply
In development

Trust for supply chains and trade.

Trade moves on paperwork that changes hands repeatedly. Kweli Supply lets each party — supplier, freight, customs, buyer — verify that the record they receive is the genuine one issued upstream.

Kweli Credentials
Future product

Verification for certificates and licences.

A credential is issued once by an institution, and anyone can verify it directly against that issuer — no phone calls, no forgeries. The same trust layer, applied to certificates, licences and professional credentials.

EAX Marketplace
Long-term direction

A trusted digital marketplace, built toward.

A marketplace where the organisations, documents and transactions are verifiable by design. This is being explored and built toward — it is not an operating marketplace today.
