Kweli Verify · the foundation
Available now

Document integrity and authenticity.

Kweli Verify lets an organisation confirm that a document is the genuine, unaltered file issued by a known organisation.

It proves three things: integrity — the file has not been changed; provenance — where the record came from; and issuer authenticity — that a known organisation issued it. It does not prove that the information inside the document is true.

Kweli Claims
Pilot focus

An integrity layer for insurance claims.

Claims decisions rest on documents from many parties. Kweli Claims adds an independent way to verify that those documents are genuine and unaltered — without replacing the claims-management systems already in use. It is designed to sit alongside existing workflows as an added layer of assurance.

Designed to support

  • Repair invoices, quotations and assessors' reports
  • Medical documents and supporting records
  • Claims evidence, attachments and audit trails
Kweli Supply
In development

Trust for supply chains and trade documentation.

Trade moves on paperwork that changes hands repeatedly. Kweli Supply is intended to let each party verify that the documents they receive are the genuine, unaltered records issued upstream — reducing the room for substituted, duplicated or altered files.

Intended to cover

  • Invoices, delivery notes and certificates
  • Inspection reports and quality-assurance records
  • Warehouse, export and supplier documents
Kweli Credentials
Future product

Verification for certificates, licences and credentials.

The same trust layer extends naturally to certificates, licences, professional credentials and institutional documents — letting recipients and third parties verify them directly. It is shown here as part of the direction, not as an immediate commercial focus.

Being explored for

  • Academic and professional certificates
  • Licences, permits and accreditation records
  • Institutional and government documents
EAX Marketplace
Long-term direction

A trusted digital marketplace, built toward — not live.

The longer-term direction is a digital marketplace in which the organisations, documents and transactions are verifiable by design — where the trust layer is present from the start rather than added afterwards.

This is being explored and built toward. It is not an operating marketplace today, and nothing on this site should be read as offering live marketplace services. It represents where the product line could lead if the earlier layers succeed.
