The thesis

Trust has to be established before decisions are made.

Digital systems are very good at helping information travel. That speed is what makes the modern economy work — and it is also the source of the problem.

The same ease that lets a genuine record move quickly lets an altered, duplicated or fabricated one move just as quickly. Nothing about a file’s appearance tells you whether it is the original.

EAX Global exists to build the layer that establishes trust before organisations act — before a claim is paid, a facility approved, a shipment accepted or a certificate honoured.

A definition

What we mean by trust infrastructure.

A shared, independent layer that records a record’s identity when it is issued and lets anyone verify a later copy against it.

It records a cryptographic fingerprint rather than the file itself, so the original never has to be exposed. It is independent of any single system, and built to be relied on before a decision, not consulted after a dispute.
