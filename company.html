Company

A technology company built to make digital evidence trustworthy.

EAX Global develops trust infrastructure for the digital economy. We are building from Nairobi, Kenya, for global markets — starting with a working product and a clear set of workflows to bring verification into next.

Mission

Establish trust before decisions are made on digital evidence.

Our mission is to give organisations an independent way to establish whether a document is genuine, original and unchanged — before they rely on it.

Our long-term ambition is to build the layer that many organisations use, across industries and borders, to verify records before capital, claims and approvals move. We start narrow and deliberately: one working product, then a set of workflows that share the same foundation.

The company exists because the cost of acting on the wrong document keeps rising, while the tools to produce convincing wrong documents keep getting cheaper. That gap is worth closing.

Where we're from

Built from Kenya, for global markets.

EAX Global is headquartered in Nairobi. We build here because it is a serious, fast-moving technology market — not as a limitation, and not as a story about any one region.

The problem we work on is global. Verifying that a record is genuine matters as much to an institution in one market as another, and we design accordingly: for organisations wherever they operate, with the same standard of engineering and the same ambition.

Principles

How we work.

Be precise about what's real

We describe what exists today plainly, and label what is in pilot, in development or a future direction as exactly that. We would rather understate than overstate.

Add a layer, don't replace systems

Organisations have working software. We build to sit alongside it and add verification, not to ask anyone to rip and replace.

Protect what we verify

Verification works from a fingerprint, not the file. Establishing that a record is genuine should not require exposing its contents.

Engineer for reliability

Infrastructure is judged on whether it can be relied on. We build for correctness and durability before novelty.

Start narrow, expand with evidence

We commit to one product working well, then extend to adjacent workflows as each earns its place.

Build in the open

We share progress honestly with partners, customers and the wider ecosystem, and invite scrutiny of what we claim.
