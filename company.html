Mission

Establish trust before decisions are made on digital evidence.

Our mission is to give organisations an independent way to establish whether a record is genuine, original and unchanged — before they rely on it.

Our long-term ambition is to build the layer that many organisations use, across industries and borders, to verify records before capital, claims and approvals move. We start narrow and deliberately.

Where we’re from

Built from Kenya, for global markets.

EAX Global is headquartered in Nairobi. We build here because it is a serious, fast-moving technology market — not as a limitation, and not as a story about any one region.

The problem we work on is global, and we design for organisations wherever they operate, with the same standard of engineering and the same ambition.

The path ahead

One product live. A clear line to a much larger platform.

Principles

How we work.

Be precise about what’s real

We describe what exists today plainly, and label pilots, development and future direction as exactly that.

Add a layer, don’t replace systems

We build to sit alongside existing software and add verification, not to rip and replace.

Protect what we verify

Verification works from a fingerprint, not the file. It should not require exposing contents.

Engineer for reliability

Infrastructure is judged on whether it can be relied on. We build for correctness before novelty.

Start narrow, expand with evidence

We commit to one product working well, then extend to adjacent workflows as each earns its place.

Build in the open

We share progress honestly and invite scrutiny of what we claim.
