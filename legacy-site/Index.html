Meet Kweli

The verification layer beneath the EAX market.

Kweli is EAX Global’s infrastructure layer for proof: hashes, timestamps, audit trails and QR-based verification without putting private data on-chain.

The marketplace is the front door. Kweli is the trust layer underneath it — built so institutions, buyers and partners can check whether a claim, certificate or record is real.

Use Cases

Built for the people who move
environmental markets.

Whether you're sourcing credits, listing projects or analysing the market — EAX is being designed with your workflow in mind.

Buyers
Companies & Institutions
Organisations seeking structured access to verified environmental assets for compliance, voluntary commitments or portfolio strategy.
  • Discover and compare assets by type, geography and standard
  • Access project-level verification signals before committing
  • Track retirement records and impact attribution
  • Receive market intelligence and pricing context
Sellers
Project Developers & Brokers
Teams with verified or developing projects who need structured market access and a credible channel to reach institutional buyers.
  • List projects with structured profiles and verification data
  • Access buyer demand signals by region and asset type
  • Manage listings and documentation in one place
  • Build credibility through transparent project records
Market
Analysts & Investors
Market participants who need reliable pricing context, asset class intelligence and structured data to inform decisions.
  • Monitor price signals across asset types and regions
  • Analyse supply and demand trends over time
  • Benchmark project performance against market
  • Access structured environmental asset data
